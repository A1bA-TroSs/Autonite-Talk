## 1. Users and demand

No group has been shown to pay for live feedback on wording and register. Demand is proven for three neighbouring jobs: delivery metrics (paid apps with thousands of ratings), live cues for call-centre agents (B2B), and Korean/English speaking practice (large consumer). Autunite's own demand is unvalidated [C28].

- **Public speaking / interviews:** Orai 4.6★ from 3.7K ratings, $12.99/mo, $49.99/yr, $99.99 lifetime (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/orai-improve-public-speaking/id1203178170, acc.). Speeko 4.7★ from 4.8K ratings, $24.99/mo, $79.99-99.99/yr (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/speeko-ai-for-public-speaking/id1071468459, acc.). Chapman 2025: 33.7% of Americans fear public speaking (via https://teleprompter.com/blog/public-speaking-statistics). YouGov 2023: 15% of Britons have a debilitating fear (https://www.anxietyuk.org.uk/?p=371833). Market sizes of USD 3.6B–7.8B (giiresearch smrc1871949, dataintelo) are unreliable. Strong for paid delivery coaching.
- **Filler / swear "jar":** Credible: Speech Coach gives live filler alerts on iPhone and Watch, verifies the speaker at setup and runs on-device; 4.4★ from 240, $9.99/mo, $39.99/yr, $99.99 lifetime (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6470220669, v1.5.0 2024-11-26). Um Detector 3.1★ from 10 (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1566958588). Swear Detector $1.99, no ratings (https://apps.apple.com/au/app/swear-detector/id6444903231). Real but small.
- **ESL (Korea):** Speak has >10M users by mid-2024 (https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/20/language-learning-app-speak-nets-20m-doubles-valuation), $78M raised at a $1B valuation (https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/openai-backed-speak-raises-78m-at-1b-valuation-to-help-users-learn-languages-by-talking-out-loud) and about $100M revenue (Benzinga 2025-11). Strong for practice; nobody coaches register live.
- **Korean learners:** Korean is #6 on Duolingo in 2025 (https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025120813084517391). King Sejong Institute enrolment was 239,020 in 2025, up from 210,374 in 2024 and 101,675 in 2020 (https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/lifestyle/trends/20260225/sejong-institutes-overseas-enrollment-tops-230000-for-first-time). Explicit honorific feedback beat implicit for non-heritage learners (https://d.lib.msu.edu/etd/1701). Medium.
- **Korean workplace:** Gapjil119 (n=1,000, 2024-09): 50.2% have been spoken to in 반말 at work, 44.4% of them felt displeasure, and 81.1% say superiors should use 존댓말 (https://www.inews24.com/view/1786751). Strong pain; no evidence anyone pays to fix their own register.
- **Customer service:** Cogito sells live agent cues (https://cogitocorp.com/product); Humana reports +28% CSAT (vendor case study). The buyer is the employer and the setting is phone calls, so it's out of scope.
- **Parents / anger:** 20% of 1,476 US parents regret their words to their children (https://preply.com/en/blog/language-of-encouragement). "I'm Not Yelling" vibrates on volume, $1.99 (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760736529). CalmParent uses Watch heart rate and tone (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6749245990). Weak.
- **Neurodivergent:** the Noora RCT (n=30) shows carry-over (https://www.psypost.org/stanford-scientists-ai-chatbot-helps-autistic-adults-improve-face-to-face-communication-studies-show/). Advocates object to masking (brocku thesis; autisticscienceperson.com 2021). Need exists; positioning risk is high.
- **HN:** speech-coach launches get 1–7 points, while a local no-cloud dictation tool got 27 points and 16 comments (HN Algolia, read 2026-11-16). (inference) "On-device" draws more interest than "coaching".
- **Privacy as a driver:** Brewer v. Otter.ai, 2025-08-15 (https://www.npr.org/2025/08/15/g-s1-83087/otter-ai-transcription-class-action-lawsuit). Yoodli's Starter and Pro plans may train on user data (https://yoodli.ai/pricing). Plaud processes in the cloud and its policy link is broken (nextpit). In Korea, Daglo had a ransomware attempt and Clova Note's MAU fell from 693K to 542K (https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260414_0003591185). (inference) "Nothing leaves the phone" is table stakes against recorders, not a differentiator among live coaches.

Patterns: complaints are opaque scores, missed or late flags, thin value and cloud training. No competitor reads Korean register. (inference) Credible is the technical twin and shows that word-list alerts alone don't earn a subscription.

## 3. UI patterns

- Sparse word-labelled cues beat continuous displays: Rhema (IUI 2015, n=30, p<0.05; https://www.hoques.com/Publications/2015/2015-Rhema-IUI-Tanveer-Lin-Hoque.pdf). One cue at a time with a minimum gap; the label matters more than the animation.
- Both live cues and post-hoc review reduce unwanted words: WSCoach (IMWUT 9(3), 2025, https://doi.org/10.1145/3749531). The recap is not optional.
- The wrist is where peers deliver cues (Credible, SpeechPacer, TalkPilot); a face-up phone may not be felt.
- Haptics are muted while recording unless `setAllowHapticsAndSystemSoundsDuringRecording(true)` is set (https://zenn.dev/fromkk/articles/c79ebea77a813b; https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/672371). Already in our code.
- A Live Activity runs up to 8 h, 12 h on the Lock Screen (https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/797676). The Watch Smart Stack mirror is silent, with no haptic (MacStories watchOS 26 review).
- Quiet visual fallback: keep the amber needle plus a word label, never colour alone.
- A recap that explains: no overall grade, each cue shows its quote and intention and can be marked accurate or not.

## 4. ASR (abbreviated)

- Apple SpeechTranscriber: iOS 26+, device-gated, a system asset, warm volatile output in 0.3–0.5 s [C20]. No published Korean CER.
- WhisperKit: MIT, iOS 17+. Sizes: tiny ~40 MB, base 80–140 MB, small 250 MB, large-v3-turbo 632 MB. FLEURS-ko CER (GGML Q8): large-v3 4.89%, turbo 5.24%, small 7.70%, base 12.98%, tiny 19.07% (https://models.handy.computer/languages/ko). On an M3 Max, hypothesis text takes 0.46 s and confirmed text about 1.7 s (https://arxiv.org/html/2507.10860v1).
- Hallucination: about 1% of 13,140 segments invented whole phrases, and 38% of those carried harms, including violent words (https://arxiv.org/html/2402.08021v2).

Recommendation: Apple stays live and Whisper is the re-pass or fallback. A Whisper-only word must never cue. Overturn this if Apple mangles Korean endings (-요/-니다/-어).

## 5. Ranked changes

1. Haptics during recording, verified on the device (S). Already in the code.
2. Apple Watch delivery (M). Conflicts with the SPEC non-goal, so it goes to Danny.
3. Cue-frequency cap and coalescing (S). "Final only" is overridden by Danny's UDP spec.
4. Cue recap with accurate/inaccurate on each cue (M), backed by WSCoach and the opaque-score complaints.
5. Practice mode, alone (M): rehearsal is what people pay for (Orai, Speeko, Yoodli), and it removes the other-voices risk.
6. User word list (S): Credible, Um Detector and Swear Detector show demand. Caveat: ASR may drop fillers.
7. Learning-Korean preset (S): English UI with Korean speech, plus learner copy.
8. Workplace presets in both directions (S): speaking to a junior colleague with 존댓말 expected (Gapjil119).
9. Live Activity for the recording state and last cue label (M).
10. Checkable privacy (S): a "Data Not Collected" label, an in-app file list and one-tap delete.
11. Voice-volume cue (M): parents (I'm Not Yelling, CalmParent). Needs its own name and evaluation.
12. Neurodivergent-safe framing (S, copy): "you set the goal".
